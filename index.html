FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

If your question is not here, it is still worth a quick call.

How often should a north Huron house have the ducts cleaned?

Work clothes, woodstoves, and gravel roads put this area on a two year rhythm for most homes, three if the house is tight and pet free. Check a return grille each spring, visible buildup means it is time.

Who exactly is coming into my house?

Certified, insured technicians. The part you can verify is the certification and the liability coverage, and both are available to read before the visit. Duct cleaning has no provincial trade designation, so those two documents are the real safeguards.

Can I see what the ducts looked like?

Yes, camera photos of your own trunk lines and branch runs, recorded before we touch anything and again after, reviewed with you on site. The footage is of your house, not a sample reel.

The dryer heats but everything stays damp. What gives?

Nine times out of ten the vent pipe is restricted. Heat without airflow just steams the clothes. We clear the run to the hood and measure the flow, and the reading tells us whether the machine itself needs a second opinion.

What does a visit involve and how long does it take?

Access plates at the plenum, the collector brought under suction, every line brushed from the register end, the blower compartment opened and cleaned, plates sealed, photos reviewed. A Wingham bungalow runs about three hours, larger homes four.

Every spring the house smells closed up when the heat comes on. Can you fix that?

That smell lives in residue along the duct walls. Full cleaning removes most of it, and a sanitizer treatment applied afterward handles stubborn must. Spring opening visits are a regular booking up here.

We clean constantly and the dust wins anyway. Why?

In the older housing stock around Wingham, unsealed return joints in the cellar keep re-supplying the system with dust no matter how often the surfaces get wiped. A pressure test finds those joints, sealing ends the cycle.

How far do you travel from Wingham?

All of north Huron, including Blyth, Brussels, Lucknow, Teeswater, Gorrie, and Fordwich, plus the farm properties between them. Distance is never a surprise, we confirm it when you book.

Do you serve areas outside of Wingham, Ontario?

Yes. We serve Wingham and nearby communities including Blyth, Brussels, Lucknow, Teeswater, and the surrounding area.
