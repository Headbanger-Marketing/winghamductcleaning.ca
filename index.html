Are loads taking longer to dry?

A dryer vent scope depends on the route from the lint-trap housing to the outdoor hood. If laundry equipment is in a basement, the run may travel horizontally or include several turns before reaching an exterior wall or roof. Photograph the dryer connection and outside termination, and note any long drying times or visible lint. Questions About Your Wingham Request

What details help with a Wingham quote?

Find out whether each grille is removed and the ductwork is brushed while a collector keeps the system under suction. This detail matters because loosened material should be drawn toward the collection equipment, rather than pushed into connected rooms. Ask which registers and return openings are part of the quoted work. If the home has accessible main trunk lines or ductwork altered during renovations, ask whether a small camera can document selected sections before and after cleaning. Images can help identify visible buildup or obstructions and clarify the limits of what was inspected. They do not show every branch unless those runs are separately reached.
